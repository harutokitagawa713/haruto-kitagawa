About

I am an incoming Master's student in the Computational and Applied Mathematics (MCAM) program at the University of Chicago (starting September 2026), where my research will focus on topological data analysis (TDA) — in particular, persistent homology and its applications to accelerating data processing.

I completed my B.S. in Mathematics (Honors) at the University of Michigan in May 2026, where I worked at the Lab of Geometry on geometric group theory, designing algorithms to compute word metrics on integer groups and the Heisenberg group, and investigating rough isometry on integer groups.

Outside of mathematics, I grew up across Japan, China (Beijing & Shanghai), and the United States, and I am fluent in Japanese, English, and Chinese.
